FAQ

Why does the session end if I leave the app?

It’s an intentional focus design: leaving the app ends the session to reduce “quick checks” and keep you present.

Does Campfire collect data?

No. Campfire has no accounts, no tracking, and no third-party analytics. Your data stays on your device.
